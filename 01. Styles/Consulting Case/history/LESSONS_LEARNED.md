## Engine / process (pilot R006, 2026-11-05)
- [E1.1] Raw is iPhone 4K HEVC 60 fps, ~1.3 GB per 3 min. Never write a 4K intermediate: disk filled mid-cut. Work at 1440x2560 (enough for the 1.3x punch-in), ~25 Mb/s videotoolbox. Check `df` before rendering (need ~3 GB free).
- [E1.2] whisper ggml-base word timestamps on a long file drift up to ~1 s; `-dtw` gives nothing with this build. What works: split audio into speech islands from a 20 ms RMS envelope (speech > −50 dB, merge gaps < 0.18 s), transcribe each island alone for text/retake detection; after the cut, transcribe each cut segment alone with `-ml 1 -sow`, then `engine/align.py` (difflib to the corrected script + onset snapping). Running 6 whisper jobs in parallel occasionally drops one output (Metal contention): retry missing ones sequentially.
- [E1.3] Rishi records a second file to redo the back half (here IMG_4599 restarts at "So who really cares…"). Treat multiple raw files as one take sequence; the later file wins for any overlapping lines.
- [E1.4] Never pipe the whole board render straight into the final encode. On R006 the Playwright→ffmpeg pipe stalled (58 min, 42 % CPU) and the renderer died at ~123 s; ffmpeg finished cleanly with a truncated reel and no error. compose.sh now renders board chunks (400 frames, 3 in parallel) to files, checks every chunk's frame count, then composites. QC's duration check is what caught it — never skip QC.
- [E1.11] Root cause of E1.4: chrome-headless-shell `page.screenshot` intermittently hangs (30 s timeout), at random frames. render.js now uses a 4 s timeout, forces a repaint and retries up to 6x. Chunks with wrong frame counts are re-rendered on the next compose.sh run (finished chunks are kept).
- [E1.12] cut.py's per-segment `fps=30000/1001` drops ~0.5 s of video vs audio over 61 segments (video 160.29 s, audio 160.78 s), so the last word played without picture. compose.sh now pads the end with `tpad=stop_mode=clone` and `-shortest` to the audio. QC must compare video vs audio stream durations (not just format duration).
- [E1.13] ffmpeg gblur via sendcmd: `sigmaV` is fixed at its initial value unless you send it too. Changing only `sigma` left a permanent vertical smear after the blur-in. Send `sigma` AND `sigmaV` every step, and bypass the filter with `enable='lt(t,…)'` once done. QC now measures sharpness at 0.05 s / 1.5 s / 3.0 s.
- [E1.14] AAC encoding adds ~0.3–0.5 dB of true peak. Limit the mix at −1.6 dBFS to land ≤ −1 dBTP in the MP4. QC checks LUFS + true peak on the final MP4.
- [E1.15] Render speed: ~0.15 s/frame normally. The 9 s/frame runs happened while the Mac idled; call request_keep_awake before long renders.
- [E1.16] Images pasted in chat don't reach the disk. Ask Rishi to save assets into 05. Asset Library/ (e.g. 05. Asset Library/stickers/mbb_logos.png) and continue the other fixes meanwhile; hook-only assets need just the S1 overlay + composite (~5 min).
- [E1.17] ffmpeg `scale=...:eval=frame` + `crop` for an animated zoom snaps size and centre to whole pixels each frame → visible wobble. Render zooms as per-frame affine transforms (PIL, bicubic, sub-pixel) instead.
- [E1.18] With the music bed, sample-peak limiting at 48 kHz left +0.1 dBTP after AAC. Limit at 4x oversampling (aresample 192k → alimiter −2.0 dB → 48k) to land ≤ −1 dBTP.
- [E1.19] The cut has almost no pauses, so a voice-keyed duck either sits permanently on (too deep: −11 dB at ratio 3) or barely moves. Calibrate the bed's level against the voice under speech (GAP 21 dB) and keep the duck light (threshold 0.1, ratio 2).
- [E1.5] Captions: split into clauses at punctuation and at scene boundaries first, then merge 1-word clauses forward, then split long clauses evenly. A greedy width-fill produced lines crossing sentences ("question. Your client is a") and a caption spanning the S2 punch-in.
- [E1.6] Number line: values closer than ~5 % of the axis (₹1,000 vs ₹1,500 on a ₹20k axis) collide. Put the reference marker (grey) below the axis, floor/ceiling on tier 1, the answer on tier 2. Keep MID working rows above y≈375 in card coords, axis at y 508.
- [E1.7] A big ✗ over a number hides the number. Strike-through line + small ✗ beside it.
- [E1.8] Face crops (1440x2560 cut, Rishi's usual framing): S1 full-bleed 1440:1560:0:360, S2 punch-in 1108:1969:166:220, S3 card 1100:1233:170:450. The first guesses clipped the hair.
- [E1.9] Hook photo: check stock candidates visually for brand logos (first Unsplash pick had AGV/Dainese marks). Record source + licence in 05. Asset Library/photos/CREDITS.md.
- [E1.10] MBB strip: no logo files in the asset library yet; R006 uses typeset wordmarks. If Rishi supplies logos, put them in 05. Asset Library/stickers/ and switch board.html #mbb to images.
