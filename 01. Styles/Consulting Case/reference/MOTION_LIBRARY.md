# MOTION_LIBRARY (v1.0)
Reference only: SKILL.md wins on any conflict.
Measured from native-fps strips (29.97 fps, 1 frame = 33 ms) unless marked [EST-sparse].

- M01 Caption word in: word placed at its final position in a pre-laid-out centred line; starts ~10–12 px lower, opacity ~0.4 (ghost), rises to baseline + full opacity over 3–4 frames (~100–130 ms), ease-out. Starts at word onset (≤1 frame early). [OBS R1 9.2–10.4]
- M02 Caption phrase change: when the next phrase starts, the whole line is replaced by a hard cut (no fade-out); new line's first word does M01. Phrases are 2–4 words (max 680 px, R1.5), broken at punctuation/clause boundaries; one line only, never 2 lines. [OBS]
- M03 Board element fade/rise: text, pills, boxes, rows: opacity 0→1 + y +8 px→0, ~200–250 ms ease-out, on the word that names them. [OBS R1 41.6–43.1]
- M04 Icon draw-on: line icons (bottle, plug, store, clock) stroke-draw ~350–450 ms, then fill fades in ~200 ms. [OBS R1 41.7–42.4]
- M05 Number count-up: currency/quantity numbers count from ~60% to final over ~400–500 ms ease-out (₹52→₹80). Ends on the spoken number. [OBS R1 42.4–43.0]
- M06 Bar grow: bars/progress grow left→right ~400–600 ms ease-in-out; years bar fills in steps synced to count (1→5 years / 15→20 years). [OBS R1 0:24–26, R2 0:31–34]
- M07 Unit grid: squares pop in sequentially ~40–60 ms apart. [OBS R1 1:13, R2 1:10] [EST-sparse]
- M08 Board clear (phase switch): all content fades out ~250–300 ms; root question box moves to top-centre. [EST-sparse R1 53–55]
- M09 Number-line marker: tick + label drop in (fade + y −8 px) ~250 ms; range between floor and ceiling fills light accent. Answer marker gets highlight strip wipe ~300 ms. [EST-sparse R1 1:10–1:28]
- M10 Tab activate: dot fills accent/navy, label goes bold, ~150 ms. [EST-sparse]
- M11 Scene cuts: S1→S2 and S2→S3 are hard cuts. No transitions anywhere else. [OBS cut detection: R1 3.00, 4.84; R2 2.47, 4.30]
- M12 Face: no zooms or moves inside S3 face card. Jump cuts in the talking head are hidden by continuous framing (no punch-in to mask them). [OBS]
