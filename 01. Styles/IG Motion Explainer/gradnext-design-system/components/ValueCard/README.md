# ValueCard

A rounded dark-glass card with a periwinkle under-glow that presents one brand value or pillar.

Provide `children` — two to four words in title case, as the values are written ("Clarity Over Complexity"). The consumer provides the dark ground (`background-04`, `gradient-02`, `rhino-950`) and the grid; cards are equal width, centred in rows of three then two.

- Corners `radius-lg`, glow `glow-periwinkle`, white Avenir Medium text.
- Only on dark grounds. No icons, emoji or numbers inside.
