How to use

Every phone is a live loop (≈5 s). Click a phone to replay it from the start. Mark each option Keep, Maybe or No, add notes per section, then press Copy my picks and paste the text to me. Your marks are saved in this browser.

What's on the board

In packoption A in each section is what the pack does today (the baseline).
NewB–F are new styles I extrapolated from your taste (smooth, premium-minimal, face as the spine, real things).
Upgradethe last section has small upgrades to motions already saved (R01, R14, R16…), shown as now vs upgrade.

What happens with your picks

Kept options get written into the pack as new IDs (MOTION_LIBRARY R26+, CAPTION_SYSTEM modes, DESIGN_SYSTEM tokens, SCENE_LIBRARY option pools) + a CHANGELOG v2.1 entry. Maybes go in the option pools only. Nothing changes until you send picks. These are sketches: behind-the-head tiles use a drawn cut-out, and the build uses the real matte.
