## Framing & captions on A-roll (split screen kept)
- Both faces stay clear. Captions sit on the **seam band (y ≈ 42–60 %)**: guest's chest and the host's wall area. That's the only zone that covers neither face.
- A soft dark gradient across the seam so white captions read. No crop, so no softness.
- To avoid 4 s+ static stretches: slow 100→104 % push on the whole frame (M06) and an alternating push-in/settle per A-roll beat.
