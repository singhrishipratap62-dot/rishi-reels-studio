How many crackers does India burst on Diwali? This is a real BCG interview question. Your client is a firecracker manufacturer planning next year's production. The CEO asks: how many crackers will India burst this Diwali? This is how you would solve it, consultant style. Before jumping to a number, I would ask clarifying questions such as: what counts as one cracker to begin with? She said, anything you light once. So for example, a phuljhadi stick counts as 1, and so does a ladi. Are we sizing all of India or just one region? She said all of India, and only for Diwali. Then I asked, is anything holding demand back? She said yes. In some states and cities now they have banned crackers, so we cannot include that. And a lot of families live in apartments where they can't burst them. So, how do we estimate it? There are two ways to go about it. First is from the demand side, counting the families who buy, or from the supply side, counting what factories make. I'll build it from the demand side and use supply to check the answer. The equation is households times share that buy crackers times crackers per household. The first thing I'll look at here is households. India has about 140 crore people. About 40 crore live in cities at 4 per home, which gives us 10 crore urban households. The other 100 crore are rural at 5 per home, so that's about 20 crore rural households. Second, we will then look at cities. Split the 10 crore homes by income into 1 crore rich, 4 crore middle and 5 crore low income class. Because of bans and apartments, I'd assume only about half the rich and middle homes buy about 40% of the low income ones, at 100, 40 and 15 crackers each, that's about 160 crore crackers from cities. Third, villages. The 20 crore homes split into 1 crore rich, 5 crore middle and 14 crore low. More of them buy: 80%, 60% and half, at 50, 30 and 10 crackers each. That's about 200 crore from villages. So, India bursts about 360 crore crackers on Diwali, and more of them go off in villages than cities. Now, the check from the supply side. At around 16 rupees a cracker, 360 crore crackers is about 6,000 crore, which is roughly what the cracker market does in a year. So the number holds up.
