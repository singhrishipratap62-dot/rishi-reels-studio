How do you

10 minutes?

Your client is

The government is
the one asking.
Its question is:
what ticket price

This is how you

Before jumping to

I would ask

what is the objective?
The client said
two things.
The company must
recover its cost
over the 30
year contract,
and the ropeway
has to stay
affordable for pilgrims.
How do people
get up today?
She said about
1 crore pilgrims

Most walk for
4 hours,
some take a pony

Then I asked,

It can only carry

There are

First, cost; second,
competition; and third,
value.
First, the cost approach.
40 lakh trips
a year for
30 years is
about 12 crore trips.
2,000 crore over 12
crore is about 170
rupees a trip.
That's our floor.
Second, competition.
Walking is free,
the pony is
1,500 rupees,
and the helicopter
is 3,000 rupees.
For anyone who doesn't
want to climb,
the pony sets
the benchmark.
Third is the value.
The ropeway saves about
4 hours of climbing,
and it's far more
comfortable than a pony.
So a pilgrim should
pay at least what
the pony costs.
That's our ceiling.
So should we charge
the floor to keep
it affordable?
No.
At 300,
about 70 lakh people
want to ride it,
but we only
have 40 lakh.
The queue at the
bottom would take hours
to clear,
and that wipes
out the very
time that ropeway
is meant to save.
At 600 rupees,
about 40 lakh
people want it,
which is exactly
our capacity.
That's less than half
the pony fare,
so it stays affordable.
And it brings in
about 240 crore a
year against a 2,000
crore cost.
