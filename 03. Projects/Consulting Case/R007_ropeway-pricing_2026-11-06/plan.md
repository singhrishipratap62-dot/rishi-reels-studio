## Arithmetic check ✔
- 40 lakh trips × 30 yrs = 1,200 lakh = **12 cr trips** ✔
- ₹2,000 cr ÷ 12 cr trips = ₹166.7 ≈ **₹170 / trip = floor** ✔
- Demand @ ₹300 = 7/10 × 1 cr = **70 lakh** > 40 lakh capacity ✔ (queue)
- Demand @ ₹600 = 4/10 × 1 cr = **40 lakh = capacity** ✔
- ₹600 < ½ × ₹1,500 = ₹750 ✔ ("less than half the pony fare")
- 40 lakh × ₹600 = **₹240 cr / yr** ✔ (vs ₹2,000 cr over 30 yrs)
- Ceiling ₹1,500 = pony fare ✔; ₹170 < ₹600 < ₹1,500 ✔
