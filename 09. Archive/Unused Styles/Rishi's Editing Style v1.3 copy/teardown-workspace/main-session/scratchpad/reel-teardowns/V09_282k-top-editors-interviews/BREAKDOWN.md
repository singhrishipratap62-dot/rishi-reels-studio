## 10. Editing psychology
| condition | action | reason [INF] |
|---|---|---|
| headline money claim | counter running before the first word, then confetti | the proof lands before any claim |
| "I interviewed X" | embedded interview player card on cream with interviewee swaps | evidence in the channel's native UI |
| each of 3 reasons | A-roll + step diamond, then a cream scene demonstrating it | the speaker introduces, graphics prove |
| a list of verbs/products | staggered bullets / carousel steps tied to spoken words ±15 f | pacing = speech pacing |
| scarcity | hand-drawn red ellipse around "20 days left" | the only red on the reel = the urgent thing |
| price | price pill appears ≈ 2.4 s before the number is said | the price is read before it's spoken (no friction) |
Pacing (state changes per 10 s [EST from §2 + A-lists]): 0-10 s: 11 · 10-20 s: 9 · 20-30 s: 9 · 30-40 s: 8 · 40-50 s: 11 · 50-56.9 s: 6. Fastest cut rhythm (hard cut every 3.56 s) with simple per-scene content.
