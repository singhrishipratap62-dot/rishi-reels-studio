## 10. Editing psychology
- Rule: abstract claim → A-roll with captions; quantity/proof ("viral edits", "how many editors", "hundreds of tutorials", "so many courses") → light canvas with many cards; accusation → stark type card. [INF]
- Pacing: hook dense (3 graphic states in 9 s), body alternates A-roll (~4 s) ↔ graphics (~2-3 s), close is calm A-roll with slow stacked captions.
- Every graphic scene ENTERS from the footage itself (footage becomes the card) — continuity keeps the speaker present.
