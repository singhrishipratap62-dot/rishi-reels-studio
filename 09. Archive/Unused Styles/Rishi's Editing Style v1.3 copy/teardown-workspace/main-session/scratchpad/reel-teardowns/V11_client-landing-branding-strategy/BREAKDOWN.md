## 10. Editing psychology
| condition | action | reason [INF] |
|---|---|---|
| proof first | account balance counts to the claimed income over the speaker | shows the outcome before the method |
| a stat ("95% of editors can't close clients") | animated percentage ring | stat becomes a gauge |
