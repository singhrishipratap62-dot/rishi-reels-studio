## 10. Editing psychology
| condition | action | reason [INF] |
|---|---|---|
| scarcity (closing forever) | say it 3 ways in 6 s: caption, calendar icon "14 days", red ellipse around "never" | repetition raises urgency without a countdown |
| price (the drop) | show the price falling, then hide it behind a mosaic | curiosity gap → comment CTA |
| product contents | portrait-card wall, then one product per spoken item (carousel, 2 cards, blueprint, AI card) | each spoken item gets a unique visual |
| fear objection (AI) | pose the objection on the face (defocus), answer with a dedicated card in a split pane | objection handled in the same breath |
| deadline | clean blue scene, text only, second red ellipse | contrast with busy walls = emphasis |
Pacing (state changes per 10 s [EST from §2 + A-lists]): 0-10 s: 12 · 10-20 s: 10 · 20-30 s: 8 · 30-40 s: 7 · 40-50 s: 8 · 50-56.4 s: 5. Densest in the hook; calmest at the deadline.

## 11. Signature moves (self-contained recipes)
1. Scarcity triple: closing-forever caption + calendar icon (pop 1.17 s `cubic-bezier(.1,.9,.2,.6)`, 14 days) + red hand-drawn ellipse (0.35 s draw).
2. Falling price then pixelation: $1,200 → $1,198 → $810 at 0.5 s steps, then mosaic blocks until the cut.
