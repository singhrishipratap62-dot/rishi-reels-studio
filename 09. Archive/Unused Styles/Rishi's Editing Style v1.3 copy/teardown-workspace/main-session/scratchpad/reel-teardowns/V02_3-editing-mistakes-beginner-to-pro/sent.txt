Here's what you should do to take your animation from beginner to profession.
Number one, stop over-sizing everything.
The biggest thing that screams beginner editor is huge icons and huge shape layers.
As a rule of thumb, the next time you're creating an animation,
make every element 25% smaller and just watch how much better it looks.
Number two, stop over-using effects.
Less is more.
The less you use in effect, the more special it is.
Reserve your visual effects for elements that you really need to grab attention.
And number three, use more dull colors.
Beginner editors always use harsh blacks, harsh whites and bright reds.
The jergiest brands always use dull colors, dull blues and dull greens.
Duller colors always look more high-end.
So now that you know that, change these three things about your edits
and watch your animations go from beginner to professional.
