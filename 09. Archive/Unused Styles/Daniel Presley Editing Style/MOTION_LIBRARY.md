## Grammar (derived)
1. **Fast in, long hold.** Entrances are 0.1–0.5 s; holds 1–4 s. [MEAS]
2. **Cuts between scene types are hard cuts.** Transitions are reserved for: name sting (M01), chapter arc wipe (M02), quote panel (M08), iris (M14). [MEAS/OBS]
3. **Build follows speech.** Chips, bullets and title words appear when the word is spoken, not on a timer. [MEAS M05/M06/M10]
4. **No camera shake, no zoom-bounce, no spin.** [OBS all 28]
