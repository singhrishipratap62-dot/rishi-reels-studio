## 3. Anti-patterns (things the refs never do) [OBS]
- Running word-by-word captions on his own speech (only R05 hook has any).
- Emoji, stickers, meme sound-effects, zoom-bounce, screen shake.
- More than one accent colour per element; gradients on text.
- Graphics that don't echo a spoken word — every card's text is lifted from what he says.
- A branded end card; endings stay calm.
- Long A-roll stretches early: first 30 s holds 6–17 cuts in every video.
