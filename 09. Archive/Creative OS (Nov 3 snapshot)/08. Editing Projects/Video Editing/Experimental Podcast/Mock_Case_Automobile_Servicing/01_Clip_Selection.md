## Top Clips by Performance Priority

1. **Reel 017** (8.6/10): A surprising, quotable claim ('Structuring is just 30, 40% of it') that challenges how most candidates prepare, plus clear direction on what to improve.
2. **Reel 012** (8.3/10): A clear diagnosis of a very common case-interview failure, followed by a reusable fix: treat brainstorming as a mini case with its own clarifying questions.
3. **Reel 011** (8.2/10): Blunt, memorable feedback ('replacing a car cannot be replaced by adding leather seat covers') that teaches candidates to sanity-check recommendations against how the business actually works.
4. **Reel 009** (7.8/10): Real interview tension: the interviewer dismantles the recommendations one by one, ending on 'What else do you think we can do?'; a moment every candidate fears and wants to see handled.
5. **Reel 014** (7.7/10): A strong, slightly contrarian business insight ('let's not play with the pricing'): servicing is typically higher-margin than car sales; this is the kind of commercial judgement Tier-1 interviewers test for.
6. **Reel 007** (7.5/10): A memorable, MECE-style '4 A's' framework delivered in about 30 seconds; highly saveable for anyone practising adoption or customer-choice cases.
7. **Reel 010** (7.0/10): An honest self-assessment ('three out of my four recommendations are something we already do') that models the reflective habit interviewers look for.
8. **Reel 003** (7.0/10): A complete, real case prompt in 40 seconds; works as a 'how would you solve this?' challenge that invites comments and saves.
9. **Reel 001** (6.6/10): A relatable candidate confession (giving laundry lists, blanking on unconventional ideas deep in the issue tree) that most consulting aspirants will recognise in themselves.
10. **Reel 006** (6.6/10): A crisp, reusable distinction between direct and indirect competition, with vivid examples (Amazon parts, DIY kits) that make it stick.
