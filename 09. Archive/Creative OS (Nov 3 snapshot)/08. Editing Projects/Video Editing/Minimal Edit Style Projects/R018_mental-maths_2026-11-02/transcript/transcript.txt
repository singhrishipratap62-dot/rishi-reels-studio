Can you work out 16% of 50 lakh in under 3 seconds?
If not, these 5 shortcuts will help you save a full minute in every case interview.
Number 1, Flip the percentage.
Because 16% of 50 lakh is the same as 50% of 16 lakh, which is 8 lakh.
So the hard version turns into an easy one.
Number 2, learn your fraction anchors, where 1/8 is 12.5% 1/6 is 16.7% and 1/7 is about 14.3%.
So if the client sells 120 out of 840 units, so that's about 14%.
Number 3, round and then correct.
So 49 times 18 becomes 15 times 18, which is 900, minus 18, which is 882.
Number 4, split awkward percentages because 15% of 640 is just 10%, which is 64, plus half
of it 32.
So 96.
Number 5, use the rule of 72 for growth.
Because dividing 72 by growth rate tells you roughly how many years something takes
to double.
So a market growing at 12% doubles in about 6 years.
None of these makes you a maths genius.
But together they let you answer while you keep talking in a case interview.
That's what sounds like confidence in a case interview.
