# SCENE PRINCIPLES — Daniel Priestley Editing Style (v1.0)

Scene types discovered across R01–R28. Frequencies are from contact sheets (one frame per cut + every 4 s, near-duplicate talking-head frames removed), so counts are rough and **over-represent graphics** [EST]. Talking head is estimated at ~55–75% of runtime [EST].

---

## S01 Talking head (A-roll) — CORE
- **Looks like**: him at a desk with sketchpad/markers; garden window behind (most), wine cellar (R08, R10), lounge sofa (R09, R11, R12). Mid shot plus a tighter punch-in on the same take. [OBS R08 00:00 vs 01:24, R24 02:09→02:13]
- **When**: default; returns between every graphic beat; carries the whole back half of long videos (R26 08:00–13:17, R28 21:34–29:16). [OBS]
- **Mistakes**: punch-in jumps that land mid-word; holding A-roll >20 s in the first 2 minutes (refs never do).

## S02 Name sting → Name card — CORE (intro signature)
- **Looks like**: dot → black diagonal band carrying "Daniel Pr" (crimson+white serif) then "Priestley" (blue) → cream card with "Daniel / Priestley" in charcoal serif, long soft shadow, letters tinted crimson/blue → diagonal wipe back to A-roll. [OBS R01 00:59–01:03, R24 00:44–00:47; full timing in MOTION_LIBRARY M01]
- **When**: once, 0:25–1:15, right after the cold-open hook + proof montage (≥24/28). [OBS]
- **For Rishi**: use the creator's own name (e.g. Rishi's/gradnext brand); keep the two-colour tint.

## S03 Chapter wipe + Chapter card — CORE (section device)
- **Looks like**: a giant cream circle edge sweeps in from the right over A-roll, carrying a white rounded-square tile with a crimson numeral; resolves to a cream card: tile at top, black serif title centred. Some videos show only the number (no title). [OBS R24 00:47, R16 00:47 "1 The Team", R24 00:48 "1 Product with Proof"]
- **When**: start of each numbered point (1–13 per video). Usually first one within 0:45–3:00. [OBS]
- **Mistakes**: un-numbered chapters; more than one title line beyond 8 words.
- **Variant**: black disc wipe then cream (R16 00:43); chapter card sliding up from bottom over A-roll (R16 00:47) [OBS].

## S04 Quote / definition card — CORE
- **Looks like**: `--paper` with giant pale ghost quote marks bottom-right; royal-blue opening mark top-left; blue Inter text, 2–4 lines, **keywords bold** in the same blue; optional italic serif attribution bottom-right. [OBS R22 00:50, R05 09:05, R17 03:47]
- **Variants**: panel wipes up over the previous scene and covers the lower ~70% (R22 00:45–00:50); centred no-mark statement (R25 02:11, R26 13:17); bold blue heading + body "definition card" (R04 00:39).
- **When**: a definition, an aphorism, a quote from someone, or the key line of a section (1–3 per video). [OBS + R22 transcript: "I believe that passion is an alignment…" 00:44 → card]

## S05 Whiteboard + PiP — CORE (most frequent graphic)
- **Looks like**: top-down real whiteboard/sketchpad plate in a light-grey rounded card on white (left ~73%), speaker PiP right with blue glow. Marker diagrams in blue/red/green, drawn live (sped up). [OBS R22 00:53, R16 06:00, R26 01:02→01:26]
- **When**: explaining a model/framework/equation step by step (5–15 per video; dominates R06, R23). [OBS]
- **Extras**: neon-blue glow trace over a drawn shape (R25 03:08); yellow highlighter swipe (R18 01:17).

## S06 List / content card + PiP — CORE
- **Looks like**: white or cream; black serif heading top-left; blue "- " bullets that build word by word; PiP right. Variants: crimson numerals + chevrons with grey sub-lines (R19 09:21, R02 21:14); red/blue filled boxes with arrows (R24 03:50). [OBS]
- **When**: enumerations ("three things", "without this mindset…" anaphora → list R22 00:21) [OBS + transcript].

## S07 B-roll card + PiP — CORE
- **Looks like**: B-roll/screenshot inside the rounded card left, same glowing PiP right, optional white "Example 1" pill bottom-left; left content hard-cuts while the PiP stays put. [OBS R18 01:28–01:33, R04 01:33]
- **When**: walking through examples while keeping the speaker present.

## S08 Name pill (person / brand / product) — CORE
- **Looks like**: white full-round pill bottom-left, circular logo/avatar, bold Inter name over small grey "Role, Company" or "2.8M Subscribers". [OBS R18 00:03, R22 00:06, R04 07:57]
- **When**: every time a real person, company or product appears on screen; in rapid montages (one per ~1.1 s shot, R18 00:03–00:05). Also "Link is in the description" (R13 15:21).

## S09 Kinetic serif title over A-roll — OPTIONAL (frequent)
- **Looks like**: white Playfair, soft glow + shadow, 1–5 words Title Case. Two placements: (a) huge across the top, **behind** his head with desaturated/cooled background + particles (R22 00:06); (b) lower third with dark gradient, words building in one at a time (R18 00:02 "How To Sell To / Rich People"). [OBS]
- **When**: the hook keyword or a coined term (0–4 per video). Variants: crimson strike-through (R11 04:29 "Special"); handwritten lead-in word above (R02 16:09); crimson highlight bar behind (R07 00:04).

## S10 Lower-third name super (no box) — OPTIONAL
- White Playfair name ~9% H + small Inter role, bottom-left, over stage/archive footage. [OBS R26 00:46, R01 26:43, R08 00:28]

## S11 Chip row (blue) / Pill stack (red) — OPTIONAL
- **Chips**: `--mist` background, black Inter header centred, blue rounded chips with white serif labels popping in one by one in a row. [OBS R24 01:33–01:41]
- **Pill stack**: same but crimson pills stacking downward (R16 05:33 "Product Ecosystem"); or red pills in a row over A-roll lower third (R08 18:33, R12 02:31).
- **When**: naming the parts of a system as he lists them, one chip per spoken item.

## S12 Icon title card / diagram card — OPTIONAL
- Cream; red/blue duotone line icon centred; black serif title; handwritten subtitle. Icons assemble from pieces. Diagram variant: handwritten labels, arrows, crimson/navy icons, yellow highlights on paper texture. [OBS R03 11:16, R20 00:57, R11 00:04]

## S13 Principle (book-page) card — OPTIONAL (R20)
- Off-white page; "Principle N" crimson serif; black serif headline; grey book body with drop cap fading out. Enters with vertical motion blur. [OBS R20 02:25] Use for book/framework-structured videos.

## S14 Framed screen / UI / clip — OPTIONAL (frequent)
- Screenshot, web page, app, YouTube page or film clip in a rounded card with soft shadow on `--mist-2` or cream; slow push/scroll; yellow highlighter on key line; brand pill bottom-left. [OBS R12 06:22, R03 08:25, R14 07:27]
- Phone mockup on white or lavender blur (R08 14:18). Chat bubble / to-do widget builds (R08 20:13, R15 06:42).

## S15 Iris spotlight + neon stat — OPTIONAL
- Circular soft-edged iris closes on A-roll to black, match-cuts inside the circle to a B&W portrait; name types in bottom-left (bold Inter + italic serif role); neon-red stat characters pop around the face and settle as "-$500,000". [OBS R24 02:20–02:25]
- **When**: a case-study person + a dramatic number (loss/gain).

## S16 Headline over B-roll — OPTIONAL
- Big white bold Inter (tight tracking), centred, ~10% H, small year line under; over full-frame B-roll in fast sequence. [OBS R27 00:29–00:33 "Dot-com crash / Global Financial Crisis 2008…"]

## S17 Data viz — OPTIONAL
- Bars, donut, progress bars (red 90% vs blue 100%), dot fields, funnels, pyramids, calendar/heatmap grids, cell grids flipping red. [OBS R01 05:36, R03 04:26, R26 00:17]

## S18 B-roll (stock / archive / film / own) — CORE
- Stock: full-frame, natural/warm, 1–3 s, fast runs (12 shots / 45 s R16 03:34). Archive: B&W or cool-desaturated stills/clips (R22 01:15–01:45). Film/TV/ads for analogies, 1–3 s (R22 02:41 Batman). Own stage/event footage for authority (R16, R17, R23 opens). Probable AI-generated story B-roll (R01, R10) [INF]. [OBS]

## S19 Photo collage / cut-outs — EXPERIMENTAL
- Face collage of rounded thumbnails on white (R22 00:10); B&W cut-out portrait with huge two-colour name (R02 19:36); polaroids sliding on cream (R02 01:23); red-blotted faces (R03 00:00). [OBS]

## S20 Light leak / glitch — EXPERIMENTAL (R22 only)
- Pink/cyan light-leak smoke over a film clip, then a dark leak frame as transition (~1.5 s); RGB/scan-line glitch ending in static (R22 00:08). Not seen in the other 27. [OBS]
