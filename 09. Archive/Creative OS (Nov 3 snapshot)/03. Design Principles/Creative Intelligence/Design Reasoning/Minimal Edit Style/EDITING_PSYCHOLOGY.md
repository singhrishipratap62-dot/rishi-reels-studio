# Editing Psychology — v1.0

## 1. The core rule
> **Announce → face. Explain → show.** `[TX 14:15]`
When the speaker introduces a new concept/category, stay on A-roll so the viewer re-connects with the person. When they describe or give an example, cut to a visual that shows it.

## 3. Scene-selection framework (run per beat)

1. **What is said?** Transcribe the beat (1 sentence or less).
2. **Core idea in ≤ 3 words.**
3. **Function:** Hook · Announce · Explain · Example/Story · Person · Number/Trend · Compare · Progression · Quote/Thesis · Emotion · Joke · Conclude.
4. **Face or visual?** Hook/Announce/Emotion/Joke-setup/Close → face by default. Others → visual.
5. **Pick the category:**

| Function | First choice | Alternates |
|---|---|---|
| Number, trend, rate, "up/down", comparison of quantities | 10 Graph | 7 Center (orbs) |
| Specific historical/romantic/philosophical image, story vignette | 3 Painting | 2 B-roll |
| Human action or felt experience | 2 B-roll | 3 Painting |
| A specific person (3rd party) | 3 Painting-character callout | 8 Side illustration |
| One abstract concept/shape | 7 Center illustration | 5A Line+illus |
| Two things at once / person + fact | 8 Side illustration | 9 Object |
| Stages, phases, process | 5B Zigzag | 4 Line+text |
| Linked abstract terms / unfolding quote | 4 Line+text | 6 Text-centric |
| Consequence, "this leads to…" | 5C Line to subject | 4 |
| Quote, thesis, conclusion | 6 Text-centric | A-roll caption |
| Personal artefact, credential, real event | 9 Object/photo | 2 |

6. **Why better than alternatives?** Write one sentence; if you can't, choose A-roll.
7. **Sync:** list trigger words and which element lands on each.

## 4. Pacing & retention
- Shot length 1.5–3 s average (measured: R1 3.1, R2 2.4, R3 2.6, R4 1.8, R5 1.5, R6 1.8, R7 1.5 s; tutorial final ≈ 2.3 s). `[EST]`
- First visual cut by ~1–2 s. `[OBS final: graph at 1 s]`
- Roughly 40–55 % A-roll, rest graphics `[EST from final]`; never > ~4 s of A-roll without a framing change. `[INF]`
- Alternate light/dark/blue backgrounds between consecutive graphic scenes to create rhythm `[OBS final: dark → paintings → white → white → dark → white → blue → white]`.
- Re-use one visual across beats when the subject continues (painting dad → painted dad) — continuity aids comprehension. `[OBS final 16–22 s]`
- Let complex illustrations breathe (≥ 2 s complete). `[TX]`

## 5. Anti-patterns
Animation for its own sake · illustrating announcements · visuals that are "about the topic" but not the line · more than one new visual idea per beat · uninterrupted graphics so long the speaker disappears (> ~8 s). `[INF]`
