# QUALITY CONTROL — Daniel Priestley Editing Style (v1.0)

Tick every box before export.

## Identity
- [ ] Fonts are Playfair Display (serif) + Inter (sans) only (+ Caveat for annotations if approved) `[R1.0]`
- [ ] Colours come from tokens only; placeholders swapped for gradnext once supplied `[R1.0]`
- [ ] Blue = idea/answer; crimson = numbers/labels/problem — no third accent except highlighter/gold in data viz
- [ ] No running captions on the speaker; no emoji, stickers, shake, zoom-bounce

## Structure
- [ ] Hook keyword title in the first seconds
- [ ] Proof beat (named people/headlines with pills) within first 30 s
- [ ] Name sting once (long-form) at 0:25–1:15
- [ ] Every numbered point opens with arc wipe + numbered tile
- [ ] At least one quote/definition card; its bold words are the nouns he stresses
- [ ] Every person/company/product on screen has a name pill or super
- [ ] Ending is calm (sign-off or CTA page), no end-card template

## Pacing
- [ ] ≥ 10 cuts in the first 30 s (16:9) / ≥ 12 (9:16)
- [ ] ASL 5–8 s overall (16:9) / 2–3.5 s (9:16)
- [ ] No pause > 0.3 s
- [ ] Graphic builds (chips, bullets, title words) land on the spoken word (±2 frames)

## Layout & motion
- [ ] PiP: right side, rounded, blue glow, stays fixed while left content changes
- [ ] Entrances 0.1–0.5 s, holds 1–4 s; between scene types = hard cut
- [ ] 9:16: all text inside x 120–960, y 250–1600; right icon column clear
- [ ] Text never over a busy area without glow/gradient; contrast passes

## Sound
- [ ] Sound added only after visuals approved
- [ ] Continuous bed ≈ 15 dB under voice; no whooshes on ordinary cuts
- [ ] Export loudness −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP

## Rights
- [ ] No third-party frames, Priestley footage or his photos in outputs
- [ ] All B-roll/music/SFX logged with source + licence
