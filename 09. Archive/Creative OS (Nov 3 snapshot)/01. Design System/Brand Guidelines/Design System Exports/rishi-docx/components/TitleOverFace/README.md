# TitleOverFace

The hook for a face-led reel: a bold title over the top of the talking-head frame for the first 2–4 seconds.

**Use for:** opening face-led explainers and stories when there's no single striking visual for a `SplitHook` ("AI agents, explained in under 2 minutes").

**Anatomy:** talking-head footage, framed a little lower (eyes at ≈42% of height) → `scrim` fade from the top down to y ≈ 760 → title at y 300–560, inside `safe-top`, left-aligned within the margins: General Sans Semibold 96px in paper, with one Gambetta italic phrase (optionally `signal`) → `Captions` as usual.

**Motion:** words arrive ≈120ms apart (rise 16px + fade, Soft, `dur-normal`) starting on your first word. Holds while you deliver the hook, then disappears with a hard cut to the first cutaway or a punch-in.

**Rules:** 3–8 words, two or three lines. Never over your face. English only, even when you speak Hinglish.

**You provide:** the title and the talking-head clip.
