# BeatWord

One big word per beat, hard-cut on the voice, over matted footage — the cinematic way to land a sentence.

**Use for:** the emotional peak of a story ("Something / completely / *ridiculous*"), a hook over footage, a chapter turn in a story reel.

**Anatomy:** footage inside a paper mat (inset 24px, 2px `line` edge) → a soft `scrim` band behind the word → the word: General Sans Semibold 150px, tracking −3%, centred, `brand-paper` → one word per sentence may be `signal` at up to 186px, filling the width → mixed lines allowed: sans word + Gambetta italic word ("for a *rupee*") → `Captions` below.

**Motion:** each word hard-cuts in on its syllable — no fade, no slide. The footage keeps its own slow push-in. Hold the last word ~0.6s, then cut.

**Rules:** one word on screen at a time (two only for a short mixed pair). Only one `signal` word per sequence. English only. Not for explaining — for landing a line you've already explained.

**You provide:** the words in order and the footage.
