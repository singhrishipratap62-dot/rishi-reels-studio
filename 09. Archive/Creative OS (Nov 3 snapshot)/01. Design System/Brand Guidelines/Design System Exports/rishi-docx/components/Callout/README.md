# Callout

A small tag connected by a hairline leader to the exact thing it names.

**Use for:** naming what the viewer should notice — "sounds robotic", "sweet spot", "AI does the work", "the whole idea".

**Anatomy:** 2px `ink` leader from the target (straight, with at most one bend) → tag: `signal-marker` bar, ink text in General Sans Semibold 32px, padding 8×16px → tiny corner crop marks around the tag.

**Motion:** leader draws from the target (Draw, `dur-quick`) → tag clips open left to right (Settle, `dur-quick`) → text is there or types in → exit reverses faster. Comes in on the word it names.

**Rules:** 2–5 words. Max two callouts on screen; the old one leaves when a new one arrives. Leaders never cross text.

**You provide:** the target and the words.
