# SplitCompare

Two columns side by side — what people think vs what's true, before vs after — with a strike and a tick.

**Use for:** myth vs reality, claim vs evidence, old way vs new way.

**Anatomy:** 4px `ink` top rule → two columns split by a `line-strong` divider → each with a `label` heading and a `headline` statement. The "wrong" side in `ink-muted` with a pen strike-through; the "right" side in `ink` with a pen tick.

**Motion:** left column in, strike draws, right column in, tick draws.

**Rules:** keep both statements short and parallel in structure. In a vertical reel, stacking (top vs bottom) also works.

**You provide:** both statements and the column labels (e.g. "What people think" / "What's true").
