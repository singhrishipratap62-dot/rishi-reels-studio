# SplitHook

The opening frame of a reel: the topic's most striking visual on the top 60%, you on the bottom 40%, a short title arriving word by word over the seam.

**Use for:** the first 1.2–2 seconds of almost every reel (all five motion references open this way).

**Anatomy:** top panel y 0–1150 (footage, result, big number, screenshot — the payoff) → 8px `bg` seam → bottom panel y 1150–1920, you framed chest-up → title in `display`, 2–5 words, one `accent-italic` signal phrase, placed in the lower part of the top panel, inside the safe area.

**Motion:** both halves visible from frame 0. Title words slide in 30px and fade, 220ms each, ≈120ms apart. Hold 1.2–2s, then hard cut (or 2-frame flash) to the full-screen visual.

**Rules:** the top half shows the answer or the shock, not a logo. Title ≤ 5 words. No captions pill during the hook if the title already says the words.

**You provide:** the top visual, your clip, and the hook title.
