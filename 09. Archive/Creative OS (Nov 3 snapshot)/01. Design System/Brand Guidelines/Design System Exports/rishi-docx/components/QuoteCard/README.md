# QuoteCard

A direct quote from a person, company, court or report, set in Gambetta.

**Use for:** when the exact words matter — a statement, a ruling, a line from a report.

**Anatomy:** `label` → large signal quote mark (Gambetta) → quote in `quote` style (Gambetta regular, 64px) → speaker in `body` Medium → source and date in `caption`.

**Motion:** quote rises in line by line; speaker and source follow 400ms later. For a long quote, reveal it sentence by sentence and add a highlighter swipe on the key phrase.

**Rules:** verbatim only, from the primary source where possible. Short — two to four lines. Quotes in another language are shown in English translation, marked "translated".

**You provide:** the exact quote, who said it, and the source.
