# HookTitle

The opening headline of a reel, carousel or cover: big General Sans with one Gambetta italic phrase in signal.

**Use for:** the first 1–3 seconds of a reel, carousel slide 1, every cover, and section titles inside longer videos.

**Anatomy:** optional numbered `label` (`01 · STORY`) → headline in `display` (two lines) or `display-xl` (one line), Semibold, tracking -2% → one key phrase in `accent-italic`, colour `signal` → optional pen underline under that phrase.

**Motion:** words rise 24px and fade in, staggered 50ms, Soft easing, `dur-normal` each. The pen underline draws 150ms after the last word lands (Draw, `dur-normal`).

**Rules:** 3–6 words per line, max 2–3 lines. Only one italic phrase. Never put the whole hook in italic or red. Must sit inside the safe area (`safe-top`, `safe-bottom`, `safe-right`).

**You provide:** the hook text (in English) and which phrase is the italic one.
