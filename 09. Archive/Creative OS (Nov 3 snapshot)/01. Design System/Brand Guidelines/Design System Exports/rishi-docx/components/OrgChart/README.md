# OrgChart

A portrait at the top with hairline branches down to framed boxes: what a person built, owns or leads.

**Use for:** who's behind a company, a founder's companies, who reports to whom, a family of products.

**Anatomy:** circular portrait with a thin ring and a Gambetta name → `line-strong` hairline branches with right angles → `MediaFrame`-style boxes with a tiny uppercase label, a logo or footage inside, and a serif-italic note under each.

**Motion:** portrait eases in → branches draw (150ms) → boxes ease in one per sentence.

**Rules:** facts only (names, years, roles) — from sources. Best on the ink theme for documentary stories.
