# RadialMap

A person or idea at the centre, with a ray burst behind it and satellites — skills, domains, products — that get connected.

**Use for:** "one person, many fields", the reach of a company, everything one AI model touches.

**Anatomy:** centre portrait or icon with a 4px ink ring → ray burst of 36 `line-strong` hairlines → 4–8 satellite circles (ink ring, ink dot) with Gambetta italic labels → optional `signal` polygon connecting them.

**Motion:** rays burst in from 60% scale → satellites pop in one per word → the signal polygon draws around them as the point lands.

**You provide:** the centre and the satellites.
