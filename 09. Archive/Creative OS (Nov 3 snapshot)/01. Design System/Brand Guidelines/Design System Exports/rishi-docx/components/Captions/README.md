# Captions

English captions that run through the whole reel, each word rising in from below as it is spoken.

**Use for:** the whole reel, over footage and over graphics.

**Anatomy:** General Sans Semibold 46px, centred, no pill or box. Dark (`ink`) text on light grounds, light (`brand-paper`) text on dark grounds; if the ground under the band changes mid-scene, the colour flips with it. Bottom edge at y ≈ 1490 (just above `safe-bottom`); the band y 1395–1490 belongs to captions alone, so every scene's own text and graphics end above y 1390. When someone else is speaking (an interview clip, a quoted voice note), the text switches to Gambetta italic.

**Motion:** each word rises in from below (about 38px over ~0.3s, ease-out), staggered ~60ms per word; the chunk fades out quickly (~0.12s) at its end time.

**Rules:** in English; for a Hinglish voiceover, a faithful English rendering of what was said, not a transliteration. 3–6 words per chunk, split on natural phrases and timed to the voice. Calm, never giant or bouncing. Updated October 2026 (replaces the old 1–3-word pill).
