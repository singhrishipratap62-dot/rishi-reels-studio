Line + text and line + illustration scenes: an accent line leads the eye from phrase to phrase or into an object; the camera follows the line head.

- Paper: `line-paper` stroke, round caps. Black: `line-dark` stroke plus a white marker dot.
- The line stops short of the text box and continues after it (M12). It never crosses letters.
- Draw 18–30 frames per segment (M11), ease-in-out; text lands as the line reaches it.
- Variants: A horizon + dot + illustration below; B zigzag through stages with alternating objects; C line straight to the subject.
- Exit: whip-blur along the line's direction (M10).
