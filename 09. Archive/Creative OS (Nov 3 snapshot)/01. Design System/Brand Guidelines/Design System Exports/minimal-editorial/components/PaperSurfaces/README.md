The five grounds: rough paper, accent blue, ink black, graph-ground with grid, and treated footage.

Every ground carries texture (see the texture tokens). Footage gets light paper grain, edge darkening (`vignette`), edge blur, and on A-roll a bottom fade (`aroll-fade`). Textures live inside the moving camera group so they move with their layer. Alternate grounds between consecutive graphic scenes. Grain must be visible on a phone; at Tesseract's default amount it wasn't [R1.1].
