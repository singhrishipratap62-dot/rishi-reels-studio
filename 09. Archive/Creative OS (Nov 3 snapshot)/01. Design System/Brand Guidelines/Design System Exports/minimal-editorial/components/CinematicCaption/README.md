Cinematic captions: ≤5 words per group, one hero word large and centred, the rest small and tucked into the gaps around it.

**Use on:** every A-roll second, and on any graphic where the line isn't already written.
**Consumer provides:** word-level timings (SRT or transcript), the hero index per group.

- Hero: on A-roll always `hero-serif`, lowercase, even for *not* [R1.1]; `hero-sans` only inside graphic scenes. On paper, serif heroes take `accent`. Max width `hero-max-width`.
- Support: `support` at 48px (≈36 % of hero), tight to a hero corner: above inset ~7 % from the hero's left, below inset ~7 % from its right. A lone trailing word sits above, on the right [R1.1].
- Vertical position: hero centre at `caption-y` (55–70 % band), inside the Reels safe zone (`safe-x`, `safe-top`, `safe-bottom`, `safe-icon-x`). Split-screen source: on the seam band over a gradient scrim.
- Motion: support words word by word on their spoken time, rising 22px while fading up (M01); hero character by character, left to right, slide + unblur (M02), done ≥350 ms before the cut; the group hard-cuts out (M04).
- Every text entry gets a quiet sound: a flick per support word, a hi-tech click per hero (Sound section).
- **Hinglish (Roman script):** the hero is the meaning word, English or Hindi (*kaam*, *dhoka*, *growth*). Helper verbs and particles (*kar diya*, *hai*, *na*) stay support size and never become the hero. Don't split a compound verb across groups. Numbers stay digits. Emotional Hindi words take `hero-serif`.
- Don't: centre every word, write full sentences, put the hero on a filler word, or place captions in the bottom 20 %.
