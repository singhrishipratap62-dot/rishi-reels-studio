Text-centric scene: the words are the visual — a quote, thesis or conclusion between two quotation marks on rough paper or blue.

- Ground: `paper` with rough texture, or `accent` full bleed (white text).
- The key phrase is `quote`/`hero`; emotional word in `hero-serif`; the lead-in is `support`.
- Quote marks appear first (M26), then the words write on with the speech.
- The marks must bracket the text: the opening mark hugs the first line top-left, the closing mark sits after the last line bottom-right. Marks far from the text read as separate elements [R1.1].
- Max ~12 words. Never forget the marks (tutorial revision #6).
