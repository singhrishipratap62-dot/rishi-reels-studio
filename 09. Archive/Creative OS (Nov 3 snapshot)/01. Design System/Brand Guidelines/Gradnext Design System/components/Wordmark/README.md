# Wordmark

The gradnext logo — a lowercase text-only wordmark — rendered from the guideline artwork as inline SVG.

Provide `tone` and `height`. Use `rhino` on `white`/`neutral-100`, `white` on `rhino`, `rhino-950`, `midnight` or brand gradients, `midnight` for the small header mark on white pages, `ink` when it must follow the theme.

- Keep clear space equal to the height of the "e" on all sides.
- Social posts: top-right at 1× padding. Presentations and video (1920×1080): corners at 1.5× padding.
- Don't stretch, rotate, italicise, capitalise, strike through or split its colour. Don't retype it in Avenir.
- For static files use the SVGs in **Logos**.
